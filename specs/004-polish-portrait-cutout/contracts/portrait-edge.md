# UI Contract: Portrait Edge

The opening screen shows one featured portrait. A visitor can check this by looking at the card.

## Edge

- No pale outline is visible around the hair, shoulders, sleeves, or hands.
- The person blends into the dark card on a phone-sized screen and on a wide screen.
- Hovering or tilting the card does not bring that outline back.
- Fine hair stays soft. It is not replaced by a hard jagged edge.

## Identity

- The portrait is still the same person in the same dark suit with the same clasped hands.
- The file shown by the card is still `src/assets/Portfolio/picture.png`.
- The picture stays 666 pixels wide and 1138 pixels tall.

## Unchanged card

- The whole head, from the top of the hair through the chin, stays inside the frame.
- The FEATURED DEV label stays readable, inside the card, and off the head.
- The opening-screen name, role, summary, and the labels View Projects, Contact Me, and Hire Me stay as they are.
